# 0010. Focus is the single currency for mismatch penalties

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-08-25

## Context

A citizen who is cold, hungry and badly matched to their trade, penalised once per cause, is penalised several times over, and the colony dies faster than anyone watching can read why. Separately, penalties that switch on at a boundary are the most-cited balance complaint in the genre, because a flip reads as arbitrary punishment rather than as pressure.

## Decision

Every unmet need and every trade mismatch resolves into one focus multiplier that scales effective stats, and output, accident chance and construction quality are consequences of that single number rather than four parallel debuffs. Focus stays independent of mood: focus drives output, mood drives the vote, and fusing them would make every vote a referendum on productivity. Each need contributes continuously to focus with a knee near zero, and the band name shown beside it is display only. Mood is two numbers, an instant target summed from active thoughts and a bar that chases it faster upward than downward, with the chase rates declared per game day rather than per game hour because at this tempo an hourly rate leaves every citizen at target.

## Consequences

One readable number explains why a citizen underperforms, and a productivity dip can be reported as foreshadowing rather than a breakdown announced after the fact. Needs keep the two thresholds of ADR 0003 for the behaviour they trigger, while the penalty curve itself is continuous and outside that decision's scope.
