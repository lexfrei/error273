# 0017. Expeditions, the leash, and dying out of sight

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-08-25

## Context

With no map edge a hauler can walk until the cold takes them, and the colony needs a reason to come back, a real chance of not coming back, and caution — none of which may be a scripted return order, because the constants already price the whole question.

## Decision

The leash is the warmth need itself: one an hour lost outside the fire's reach and two an hour regained inside it, from a ceiling of a hundred to a death at zero, which makes an out-and-back excursion cost twice its depth and puts the colony's reach at fifty cells past the freezing line. Caution is the lookahead a fixed threshold does not have, so warmth's acting mark becomes the cost of getting home plus a margin under ADR 0003, with the margin a universal constant shifted only by the tradition cards a citizen carries, and a patch is worked down further the farther it is because travel counted as dead time is what sets the give-up point. Discovery is a deliberate scout drawn from citizens the ballot's survival tier has nothing to say about, walking a heavy-tailed step distribution drawn from seeded noise. A waystation is the fourth building on the ballot, elected by the same detour hours that already elect a boiler and sited where those detours concentrate, running on a fuel line into a small on-site cache so that a chain of them is a standing tax on the workforce that grows with its length.

## Consequences

A citizen who does not return is recorded as missing rather than dead: the colony learns only that they did not come back, and closing the event costs either a second citizen the same walk to recover the body or the build capacity to raise a memorial without one. Sending a hauler further is right for a colony short of stores and wrong for a citizen short of warmth, so the two budgets disagree by construction and the citizen who dies is the one a hungry colony sent on a thin day; the expedition layer is therefore only worth its weight if the risk and reward gradient of ADR 0016 makes a distant trip beat staying home, which the balance log has to show.
