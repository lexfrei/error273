# Balance log

One row per commit that can move the shape of a run, so a tuning change that quietly flattens the arc is visible as a diff rather than a memory.

Rows are keyed by commit subject rather than hash: a commit cannot contain its own hash, and a rebase invalidates one written after the fact.

**The last-death column changed meaning at `feat: a citizen who does not come back is missing`.** Before that row it holds one number, taken on the world seed the game ships with. That turned out to measure the seed rather than the change: the world a colony is founded in dominates how long it lasts, and on four seeds the same build ran 240, 494, 30 and 3 years. A single trajectory of a system where one unit of food in year 28 decides a three-hundred-year difference is not evidence about a rule. From that row the column carries four figures -- the last year reached on world seeds `0x2026`, `0x2027`, `0x2028` and `0x2029`, the shipping one first so the older rows still line up beside it. Rows written before the change are left as they were rather than restated, because what they measured is what they say they measured.

**A figure with a `+` after it is a colony that outlived the instrument**, not one that died in that year: the run was cut by the wall clock with the colony still going, so the number is where the measuring stopped. It is the difference between an event and a censored observation and the column carries it rather than leaving it to be remembered. A censored figure never counts as a death in any median quoted over a mixed set; where one is quoted, how many were censored is said with it.
