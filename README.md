# error273

A zero-player game. A generator burns wood in the middle of a frozen disc, thirty citizens haul more wood in from the treeline, and you watch. There is no input and no way to intervene. The forest is finite, so every run ends with the colony frozen. Rust on Bevy 0.19, running headless (`default_app`, no window, no GPU); the only output is ASCII redrawn to the terminal with ANSI escapes.

The line under the map is the tick counter, living citizens, wood sitting in the generator, and wood left standing in the forest.

## Running it

Needs a stable Rust toolchain.

```bash
rustup toolchain install stable
cargo run
```

It redraws in place about twelve times a second and exits on its own once everyone is dead. Ctrl+C ends it early.

```bash
cargo test
```

## What the simulation does

The map is a disc of radius 18. The generator sits at the centre, the forest on the rim.

Ambient is -30 everywhere. The generator adds 66 on top of that at its own cell and 3 less for every cell of distance, so the middle of the map sits at +36 and the air reaches zero about twelve cells out. With the fuel at zero the generator adds nothing and the whole map is ambient.

Citizens gain 2 warmth per tick in the heat and lose 1 per tick in the cold, out of 100. At zero the citizen is removed from the simulation.

Hauling is the whole economy. A citizen walks to the nearest forest cell that still has wood, takes one unit, carries it back to the generator and drops it in as fuel. The generator burns one unit every fourth tick.

Warming up interrupts hauling. Below 25 warmth a citizen drops the trip and heads for the generator, and does not go back out until warmth is up to 75. The gap between the two thresholds keeps citizens from oscillating at the edge of the warm zone.

Eight forest cells, 40 units each, 320 in total, and nothing regrows. So the wood runs out, the generator goes cold, and everyone freezes.

Everything runs on `MinimalPlugins` with a fixed 80 ms loop: advance the tick, run citizen decisions, burn fuel, redraw.
